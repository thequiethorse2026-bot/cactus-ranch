Shipping Information

Whether you're ordering Western art prints, books, or ranch‑themed goods, this page explains how your items make their way from the DM Ranch to your doorstep.

1. Processing Time

Orders are usually processed within 2–4 business days. Custom prints or special items may take a little longer, but I’ll always keep things moving.

2. Shipping Times

Delivery times depend on your location and the carrier. Typical estimates:

• United States: 5–10 business days
• Canada: 7–14 business days
• International: 10–21 business days

3. Shipping Costs

Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on weight, size, and destination. I do not add hidden fees — what you see is what you pay.

4. Tracking Your Order

Most orders include tracking. Once your package ships, you’ll receive an email with your tracking number.

5. Lost or Delayed Packages

If your package is delayed or appears lost, contact me and I’ll work with the carrier to track it down. If it can’t be found, I’ll replace the item or issue a refund.

6. Damaged Items

If your order arrives damaged, send a photo to thequiethorse2026@gmail.com and I’ll replace it according to the Refund Policy.

7. International Orders

Some countries may charge customs fees or import taxes. These are the responsibility of the buyer and vary by region.
