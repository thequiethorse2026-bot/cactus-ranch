Shipping Information

Everything you need to know about how your Western art and books make their way from the ranch to your doorstep.

Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 3–5 business days. Each print is inspected and packaged with care to ensure it arrives in perfect condition.

Shipping Methods

All orders are shipped via USPS or UPS, depending on destination and package size. Tracking information is provided for every order.

Domestic Shipping

Most U.S. orders arrive within 5–10 business days after processing. Rural areas may take slightly longer.

International Shipping

International shipping is available. Delivery times vary by country and may take 2–4 weeks depending on customs processing.

Packaging

Art prints are shipped flat in protective rigid mailers. Books are shipped in padded envelopes or sturdy boxes depending on quantity.

Lost or Damaged Orders

If your order arrives damaged or does not arrive at all, contact me at thequiethorse2026@gmail.com and I’ll make it right.

Returns & Refunds

Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery for unused, undamaged items. Contact me to begin the process.
