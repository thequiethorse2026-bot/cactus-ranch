Refund & Return Policy

Your satisfaction matters — and I stand behind every piece I sell.

If Something Goes Wrong

Every print and product is made with care. If your order arrives damaged, bent, misprinted, or incorrect, I’ll make it right — no hassle, no runaround.

Damaged or Lost Packages

If your package gets lost on the trail or arrives in rough shape, contact me within 14 days of delivery. I’ll replace the print or issue a refund.

Returns

Because each print is made fresh when you order it, I don’t accept returns for buyer’s remorse. But if there’s a real issue with the product, I’ll fix it.

Digital Downloads

Digital items can’t be returned, but if you have trouble accessing your files or something isn’t right, I’ll resend the download or help you get it working.

Original Artwork

Originals are one‑of‑a‑kind. If an original arrives damaged, I’ll work with you on a fair solution — refund, repair, or replacement if possible.
