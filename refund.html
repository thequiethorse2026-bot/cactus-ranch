Refund & Return Policy

I stand behind every piece of Western art, every book, and every item sold through the Cactus Ranch. If something goes wrong, I’ll make it right.

1. Damaged or Defective Items

If your order arrives damaged, misprinted, or defective, contact me within 14 days of delivery. I’ll replace the item or issue a refund. Photos of the damage help speed things along.

2. Lost Packages

If your package never arrives, I’ll work with the carrier to track it down. If it can’t be found, I’ll replace the item or refund your purchase.

3. Digital Downloads

Digital items (art files, guides, etc.) cannot be returned. But if your file is corrupted or won’t download, I’ll resend it or provide a working version.

4. Physical Prints & Books

Physical items are printed and shipped by trusted partners. If the item arrives damaged, I’ll replace it. Returns for buyer’s remorse are not accepted.

5. Original Artwork

Originals are one‑of‑a‑kind. If an original arrives damaged, I’ll work with you on a fair solution — refund, repair, or replacement if possible.
