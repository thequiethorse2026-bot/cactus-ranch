About Daniel McKagan

My name is Daniel McKagan, and Cactus Ranch is where I bring Western stories, artwork, and frontier grit to life. From mustang silhouettes to campfire tales, everything here is built with the same spirit that shaped the old American West.

I create Western art in charcoal, pencil, and digital mediums, blending realism with the rugged atmosphere of the frontier. My stories follow characters who walk the hard trails — men and women shaped by dust, danger, and the quiet moments that define a life.

Whether you're here for the gallery, the books, or the Trading Post, I hope you find something that speaks to your own love of the West.
