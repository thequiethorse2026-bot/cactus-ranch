About the Artist

The Cactus Ranch is the home of Western art, frontier storytelling, and the grit of the open range. Every piece here is created by hand, shaped by memory, and rooted in the spirit of the American West.

Daniel McKegan

I’m Daniel McKegan — artist, storyteller, and creator of the Cactus Ranch Gallery. My work blends charcoal, pencil, and digital brushwork to capture the dust, danger, and quiet beauty of the frontier. Whether it’s a lone mustang at sunset or a gunslinger facing down trouble, I aim to bring the West back to life one scene at a time.

The Mission

The goal of the Cactus Ranch is simple: create Western art and stories worth someone’s hard‑earned money — especially in tough times. Every book, every print, every sketch is made with care, honesty, and respect for the people who still believe in the old ways.

The Stories

I write the Troubleshooter Series, following Neal Doleson through a world of frontier justice, dusty trails, and hard choices. More books are on the way, and each one aims to be bigger, sharper, and worth every penny.

The Art

My preferred style is charcoal and pencil — raw, gritty, and honest. Every piece is meant to feel like it could’ve been pulled straight from a saddlebag after a long ride across the basin.
