📄 Image Checklist

A step-by-step checklist for adding 20–30 images safely.

  • Add images to /images
  • Rename cleanly
  • Optimize sizes
  • Add to gallery or books
  • Update image index

🔤 Rename Guide

Clean naming rules for large batches of images.

  • Lowercase only
  • Use hyphens
  • Short names
  • Use categories: gallery-, book-, banner-
  • Use numbers: mesa-01, mesa-02

⚙️ Optimization Guide

Keep your site fast by shrinking images before upload.

  • Banners → 1600px wide
  • Gallery → 1200px wide
  • Books → 1000px tall
  • Export JPG at 80% quality

🖼️ Gallery Template

Paste this block into gallery3.html to add many images at once.

<!-- BEGIN NEW GALLERY IMAGES -->
<img src="images/gallery-mesa-01.jpg" alt="Mesa 01">
<img src="images/gallery-mesa-02.jpg" alt="Mesa 02">
<img src="images/gallery-canyon-01.jpg" alt="Canyon 01">
<!-- END NEW GALLERY IMAGES -->
